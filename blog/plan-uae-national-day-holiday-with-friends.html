How to Plan a Memorable UAE National Day Holiday With Friends

Make the most of the UAE's National Day break with destination ideas, group travel tips, road trips and memorable experiences.

Home / Blog / UAE National Day Holiday With Friends
Travel Guide | UAE | National Day Travel

UAE National Day is one of the best excuses of the year to get your friends together. The weather is finally pleasant, the whole country is decked out in red, green, white and black, and there are fireworks, heritage events and road-trip routes in every direction. The trick is planning early, because everyone else has the same idea.

This guide walks you through it: when the holiday falls, how to choose a destination, split costs fairly and book a place to stay, and what to do once you get there. If you'd rather skip the logistics, National Day tour packages can bundle hotels, transfers and activities so your group spends less time organising and more time celebrating.

When Is UAE National Day in 2026?

The holiday is officially called Eid Al Etihad, and 2026 marks the 55th National Day. It falls on Wednesday 2 and Thursday 3 December. No change had been announced when this was written, but the Cabinet can move non-Eid holidays to the start or end of the week, so confirm the official announcement before you book.

Because the days fall mid-week, one day of leave on Friday 4 December turns it into a five-day break with the weekend. Hotels and flights get pricey fast for a window like that.

Plan Ahead With Your Friends

A group trip lives or dies on the first conversation. Ask everyone what they want from the trip before anyone books anything. Some people want fireworks and nightlife, some want a quiet beach, and others will happily trade a lie-in for a sunrise hike.

Agree on dates, how you'll get around, where you'll stay and roughly what you'll spend. A short group chat poll takes ten minutes and saves a lot of awkwardness later.

Choose the Right Destination

The UAE gives you plenty of choice, so match the place to the group.

  • Dubai: Suits groups who want entertainment, shopping, restaurants and big celebrations. Think Downtown, Dubai Marina and the beach clubs.
  • Abu Dhabi: Best for culture and calmer days, with Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque, Qasr Al Watan and Saadiyat Island.
  • Ras Al Khaimah: The pick for hikers and adventure fans, with the Jebel Jais mountains and its famous zipline.
  • Fujairah: Ideal for beach days and snorkelling on the east coast, especially around Snoopy Island at Al Aqah.

If your group can't agree, base yourselves in one city and add day trips.

Set a Budget Everyone Is Comfortable With

Money is where group trips most often go wrong, so talk about it early. Ask each person what they're willing to spend on accommodation, transport, food and activities.

Share what's naturally shared, such as hotel rooms, a rental car, petrol and group meals. Keep personal shopping and optional activities separate. Add a buffer for parking, last-minute bookings, snacks and entry tickets, which add up quickly. A clear budget means nobody ends up quietly stressed about the bill.

Catch the Fireworks

Fireworks are the headline of National Day, and they make great group photos. Displays happen across the emirates, and waterfronts and entertainment districts fill up fast.

Venues and times for 2026 hadn't been announced when this was written, so check official sources before you go. As a guide, last year's shows included Global Village in Dubai (nightly at 9 pm over 1 to 3 December), The Beach at JBR, Bluewaters and Souk Al Seef (9 pm on 2 December), and Hatta (8 pm on 2 December, free). In Abu Dhabi, Yas Bay and the Corniche and Emirates Palace area were the main spots.

Get there early for a good viewing spot, bring water, and plan your way home in advance, since roads and car parks around waterfronts get jammed afterwards.

Explore Emirati Culture and Heritage

National Day is about more than fireworks, so build in something that tells the story behind the celebration. Rather than a typical weekend, try a heritage experience.

In Dubai, wander the Al Fahidi Historical Neighbourhood, with its traditional architecture and small museums. Hatta Heritage Village is a restored mountain village that's free to enter. In Abu Dhabi, Qasr Al Hosn is the oldest stone building in the city, and Heritage Village near the Corniche has craft demonstrations. You'll learn how pearl diving, fishing and desert life shaped the country.

Many of these sites run special cultural performances on National Day, though 2026 programmes may not be confirmed yet.

Plan a Road Trip

The UAE's roads make short group road trips easy. Hatta is about 130 km from Dubai, roughly 90 minutes by car, with mountain scenery and plenty to do on arrival. For the coast, head to Fujairah instead.

If you've got two days, try a loop through Wadi Shees, Al Rafisah Dam, Khorfakkan and Dibba, then snorkel at Snoopy Island and stop at Al Bidya Mosque, the UAE's oldest, on the way back. It's around 400 km in total.

Before you go, check your tyres, fuel and navigation. Share the driving and take breaks. Holiday traffic can add a lot of time, so leave early and don't schedule the day too tightly.

Get Outdoors

If your group likes to stay active, December is the ideal time. The weather is cooler, and there's plenty to do.

Hatta Dam is a favourite for kayaking, and the area also has hiking trails and mountain biking. Near Dubai, you can go on a desert safari or try dune bashing. On the east coast, Fujairah has clear water for snorkelling and swimming. Ras Al Khaimah has the Jebel Jais mountains for hiking and adventure activities.

A beach picnic is an easy, cheap way to fill an afternoon. Pack snacks, drinks and a few games, and stick to public areas where you're allowed to set up.

Book Your Accommodation Early

Where you stay shapes the whole trip. National Day is one of the busiest staycation periods of the year, and hotels in Fujairah and Dibba tend to fill up first. Rates generally climb as availability shrinks, so early booking is the best way to save.

Choose a base close to your main plans, with restaurants and transport nearby. For bigger groups, look at adjoining rooms or a holiday apartment with a shared living area. Compare prices, cancellation policies and recent reviews, and look out for festive packages that include meals or activities.

Eat Well Together

Group meals are some of the best parts of a trip. Try traditional Emirati dishes such as machboos (spiced rice with meat), harees (slow-cooked wheat and meat), luqaimat (sweet dumplings) and Arabic coffee. Plenty of restaurants run special menus around National Day.

Book tables for larger groups, and check everyone's dietary needs beforehand. If you've got an apartment with a kitchen, a simple home-cooked meal or picnic is a cheap and fun change.

Take Photos, but Stay Flexible

Capture the group, the fireworks and the scenery, then put the phones away and enjoy it. A shared photo album lets everyone add their favourites afterwards.

Stay flexible, too. Traffic, weather and event timings can all change. Don't pack every day with attractions, and leave room for rest and spontaneous detours.

Conclusion

A great National Day trip with friends comes down to a few things: agree on the plan together, set a realistic budget, book early and leave room to improvise. Whether you're after fireworks, heritage, a mountain road trip or a beach day, there's a lot of ground to cover.

Whether you want a city break, a mountain escape or a quiet staycation, the aim is the same: shared experiences that everyone enjoys. A National Day tour package can handle the bookings and let your group focus on the fun.
