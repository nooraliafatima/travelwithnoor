Best International Holiday Ideas for Couples During UAE National Day

From peaceful tropical islands to charming European cities, discover destinations where couples can make the most of the UAE National Day break.

Travel With Noor · UAE National Day Travel Guide · 2026

Eid Al Etihad, the UAE National Day holiday, falls on Wednesday 2 and Thursday 3 December 2026. Although this is a short break on paper, it can be enough for a memorable trip with your partner. You could extend your holiday with annual leave if your schedule allows. Any additional public holiday depends on an official announcement, so check the dates before making non-refundable bookings.

The right destination depends on how many days you have, your budget, the weather you prefer and the experiences you want to share. Couples can choose from tropical beaches, historic cities, snowy mountains or relaxed island escapes. For a three to five-day break, destinations with shorter flights from the UAE can help you spend more time exploring and less time travelling. Longer journeys, such as Bali or some European trips, are generally better when you can take a full week.

December is a busy travel period, so compare flight times, hotel locations, transfer costs and entry requirements early. If you prefer help coordinating your trip, you can also explore UAE National Day travel options and check the exact inclusions before booking.

1. Maldives: A Private Escape by the Sea

If you want privacy, beautiful beaches and a slower pace, the Maldives is an appealing choice for couples. Picture turquoise lagoons, white sand and villas overlooking the water. You can spend the day swimming, enjoying a couples' spa treatment, having a long lunch or watching the sunset together.

There is more to do than relax on the beach. Depending on the island and resort, you can arrange a sunset cruise, snorkelling trip or private dinner. Resorts can be expensive, so some travellers consider guesthouses on local islands instead. Keep in mind that alcohol is generally available at resorts but not on local islands.

Before booking, compare boat or seaplane transfers, meal plans and accommodation costs. December is usually within the Maldives' drier season and is a popular time to visit, so availability can be limited. Check current entry requirements for your passport, including any accommodation and onward-travel documentation.

2. Georgia: Culture, Food and Mountain Air

Georgia is a good option for couples who enjoy historic streets, local food and a mix of city life and mountain scenery. In Tbilisi, explore the Old Town's narrow lanes, admire its colourful balconies and visit the Abanotubani sulphur bath district. Cafés and traditional restaurants provide plenty of places to take a break between walks.

Make time to try Georgian dishes such as khinkali dumplings and khachapuri cheese bread. An evening walk beside the Kura River is another simple way to enjoy the city together.

If you have more time, consider travelling north to Kazbegi for mountain views and small villages. Early December can bring snow and freezing conditions, and road conditions may change quickly. Leave flexibility in your itinerary and check local travel conditions before setting off.

Flights from the UAE are relatively short compared with many European destinations. Entry rules depend on nationality and residence status, so verify the current requirements for your passport before departure.

3. Paris: A Classic Romantic City Break

Paris remains a favourite for couples who enjoy art, architecture, food and long city walks. Visit the Eiffel Tower, spend time at the Louvre, explore Montmartre and settle into a French bistro for dinner. Booking timed entry for major attractions can help you make better use of a short holiday.

An evening cruise on the Seine offers another view of the city's bridges and illuminated landmarks. In early December, the weather is cold, so pack warm layers and comfortable shoes. Seasonal decorations and Christmas markets can also add a festive atmosphere to the visit.

Most UAE residents who do not hold an EU or other visa-exempt passport need a Schengen visa to visit France. Check the rules that apply to your nationality and allow enough time for the application. Staying near a metro station can make it easier to reach the main attractions.

4. Switzerland: Mountains, Lakes and Scenic Trains

Switzerland is an attractive choice for couples who dream of mountain views, peaceful lakeside walks and cosy villages. Lucerne, Interlaken and the Jungfrau region are popular places to use as a base. Depending on conditions, you can take a scenic train, visit a mountain viewpoint or spend an afternoon exploring a town beside the lake.

Snow is possible at higher elevations in early December, but conditions vary by location. Lower lakeside towns may not have snow, and some mountain lifts or attractions may close temporarily for seasonal maintenance. Check operating schedules before building your plans around a particular excursion.

Switzerland can be expensive, especially when you add accommodation, trains and mountain activities. Compare transport passes, stay close to public transport and choose a few special experiences rather than scheduling a costly activity every day. Visa requirements depend on your nationality and should be confirmed before travel.

5. Bali: Beaches, Rice Terraces and Wellness

Bali offers couples a choice between lively beach areas and quieter inland retreats. Seminyak and Canggu are known for cafés, restaurants and sunset spots, while Ubud is popular for its green surroundings, rice terraces and wellness experiences.

You could book a traditional spa treatment, visit a temple, join a cooking class or spend time walking through the countryside. Some couples prefer a beach-focused stay, while others choose a few nights inland to enjoy a different side of the island.

Bali's rainy season generally runs from around November to March, making December a wet period. Heavy showers can affect outdoor plans, so keep some flexibility and consider indoor activities as a backup. Choose your accommodation carefully because travelling between areas can take longer than expected.

The journey from the UAE is considerably longer than a trip to Georgia or Istanbul, so Bali is usually more suitable if you can take several extra days. Check current Indonesian entry requirements and the local tourist levy before departure.

6. Prague: Cobblestones and Christmas Markets

Prague is ideal for couples who enjoy historic architecture, cosy cafés and scenic city walks. Cross Charles Bridge, explore Old Town Square, watch the astronomical clock and visit Prague Castle. The city's historic centre is recognised as a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Christmas markets often open around late November or early December. Depending on the year's schedule, you may find festive decorations, seasonal food, handmade gifts and warm drinks in the main squares. Check official event dates before travelling because opening schedules can vary.

Early December is cold, so pack a warm coat, gloves and comfortable shoes for walking on cobblestones. A hotel in or near the historic centre can reduce commuting time. Visa requirements depend on your passport; many travellers need a valid Schengen visa.

7. Seychelles: A Quiet Tropical Island Break

Seychelles is worth considering if you and your partner prefer tropical scenery and a relaxed holiday. The islands are known for granite boulders, clear water, palm-lined beaches and green hills. Mahé is a common starting point, while Praslin and La Digue offer other landscapes and experiences.

Spend your time snorkelling, walking nature trails, taking a boat trip or simply enjoying the beach. Accommodation ranges from luxury resorts to smaller guesthouses, allowing couples to choose a style that suits their budget.

December is generally warm and humid and can bring showers. Allow flexibility in your plans, particularly if you are arranging boat excursions or travelling between islands. Compare ferry schedules, transfer costs and accommodation locations before finalising your stay.

Check the required travel authorisation and entry conditions for your nationality before flying. Do not assume visa-free entry means that no other travel documents are required.

8. Istanbul: Two Continents in One City

Istanbul brings together European and Asian influences through its architecture, food, markets and neighbourhoods. Explore the historic Sultanahmet area, visit the Blue Mosque and Hagia Sophia, and browse the Grand Bazaar for textiles, pottery, jewellery and souvenirs.

Hagia Sophia is an active mosque, so check current visitor arrangements, ticketing requirements and prayer-time restrictions before your visit. A Bosphorus cruise is another memorable way to see the city's waterfront and skyline.

Turkish cafés and restaurants offer plenty of opportunities to try local dishes and desserts together. December can be cold and rainy, so combine outdoor sightseeing with museums, cafés or a traditional hammam. Istanbul may be more budget-friendly than some Western European capitals, although costs depend on your hotel and activities.

Turkish entry requirements vary by nationality. UAE citizens and foreign residents may have different rules, so check the requirements for your own passport rather than relying on UAE residency alone.

9. Sri Lanka: Nature, Tea Country and Train Rides

Sri Lanka suits couples who want variety without trying to fit every day around a city itinerary. Depending on the length of your stay, you can explore Kandy, visit ancient sites such as Sigiriya, take a train journey through tea country or spend a few days on the coast.

The south and southwest coast, including places such as Galle, Mirissa and Bentota, are often appealing in December. The hill country is cooler and can experience rain, while conditions on the east coast are less reliable at this time of year. Check local forecasts when choosing the order of your stops.

Travel between regions can take longer than a map suggests. If you want to visit several areas during a short break, plan realistic transfer times and consider using a reliable driver. This helps you spend more time enjoying each place instead of rushing between them.

Check the current Sri Lankan entry process and any electronic travel authorisation requirements that apply to your passport before departure.

Tips for Planning a Couple's Holiday During UAE National Day

Choose a destination that fits your available days

A short holiday is easier when you avoid spending too much of it in airports or on long transfers. For a three to five-day break, compare shorter flights and direct connections first. Save destinations with longer journeys for a week or more whenever possible.

Set a shared budget

Agree on a budget before you book. Include flights, accommodation, meals, transport, activities, travel insurance and any visa or authorisation fees. A destination with cheaper flights may not be cheaper overall if local transfers and hotels are expensive.

Check the December weather

December can mean snow in mountain regions, chilly weather in European cities or tropical rain in parts of Southeast Asia. Choose activities that suit the expected conditions and keep a backup plan for outdoor experiences.

Confirm visa and entry requirements

UAE residency does not automatically provide the same entry privileges as a UAE passport. Check the official immigration guidance for your nationality and travel document, and confirm whether a visa, electronic authorisation, insurance or other documentation is required.

Leave room for unplanned moments

You do not need to fill every hour with sightseeing. Leave space for a slow breakfast, a scenic walk or a restaurant you discover along the way. A realistic itinerary often makes a couple's holiday more enjoyable than trying to see everything in a few days.

Before you book: Confirm the official holiday dates, flight schedules, accommodation cancellation rules, attraction opening times and entry requirements. Check what is included in any travel arrangement so you can compare options fairly.

Conclusion

The UAE National Day holiday offers couples a chance to explore somewhere new and enjoy time together. The Maldives is suited to a peaceful beach escape, Georgia offers culture and mountain scenery, Switzerland brings alpine landscapes, and Prague adds a festive winter atmosphere. Bali, Seychelles and Sri Lanka are appealing for tropical scenery, while Paris and Istanbul offer memorable city experiences.

The best choice depends on your interests, budget, available days and the weather you want. Compare travel times and total costs, check entry rules early and avoid packing too many stops into a short holiday.
