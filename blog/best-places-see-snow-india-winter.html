India Winter Travel Guide

Best Places to See Snow in India During Winter

Discover India's beautiful snowy destinations, from Himalayan valleys and peaceful hill stations to popular skiing locations and mountain escapes.

Travel With Noor · India Winter Travel Guide

Imagine waking up to beautiful snow-covered mountains, enjoying a cup of hot coffee in chilly weather and watching snowflakes fall around you. India in winter offers a memorable experience for travellers who enjoy snowy landscapes, mountain adventures and peaceful holiday destinations.

From the dazzling valleys of Kashmir to the charming hill stations of Himachal Pradesh and Uttarakhand, India has several beautiful places where visitors can experience the magic of winter.

Whether you are planning a romantic holiday, a family vacation, an adventure trip or a getaway with friends, exploring the places to see snow in India can make your winter holiday memorable.

Choosing the right destination is important if you want to experience snowfall. Some places are popular for skiing and snowboarding, while others offer mountain scenery, peaceful surroundings and seasonal outdoor activities.

1. Gulmarg, Kashmir – A Paradise for Snow Lovers

Gulmarg is one of India's most beautiful winter destinations. Surrounded by snow-covered mountains and pine forests, the town becomes a spectacular place to visit during the colder months.

It is particularly popular for skiing and snowboarding. The Gulmarg Gondola is another major attraction, offering impressive views of the surrounding Himalayan landscape.

Top Things to Do

  • Enjoy skiing and snowboarding when conditions permit.
  • Experience the Gulmarg Gondola.
  • Take photographs of snowy mountain scenery.
  • Try seasonal snow activities with suitable operators.

Best time to visit: December to March, depending on snowfall and local conditions.

2. Manali, Himachal Pradesh – A Favourite Winter Getaway

Manali is one of India's popular winter holiday destinations. Its mountain scenery, charming cafés and seasonal snow attract travellers throughout the winter months.

Solang Valley is a favourite place for snow activities when weather and snow conditions are suitable. Visitors can also explore Old Manali and the famous Hadimba Devi Temple.

Top Things to Do

  • Enjoy seasonal snow activities at Solang Valley.
  • Explore the streets and cafés of Old Manali.
  • Visit Hadimba Devi Temple.
  • Try local Himachali food and enjoy mountain views.

Popular winter period: December to February.

3. Shimla, Himachal Pradesh – Enjoy a Snowy Hill Station

Shimla is a charming hill station known for its colonial architecture, mountain scenery and winter atmosphere. When snowfall occurs, the town's rooftops and surrounding forests can create beautiful snowy views.

Kufri, located near Shimla, is especially popular among visitors looking for seasonal snow activities and panoramic mountain scenery.

Top Things to Do

  • Walk along Mall Road.
  • Visit The Ridge.
  • Explore Kufri when snow conditions are suitable.
  • Enjoy local shopping and regional food.

Best time to visit: December to February, depending on snowfall.

4. Auli, Uttarakhand – The Dream Skiing Destination

Auli is one of India's well-known skiing destinations. It is surrounded by snowy landscapes and impressive Himalayan mountain scenery.

The destination attracts travellers interested in winter sports and mountain views. The Auli Ropeway is another popular attraction, offering scenic views of the surrounding landscape.

Top Things to Do

  • Enjoy skiing and snowboarding with suitable conditions.
  • Ride the Auli Ropeway when operational.
  • Discover scenic mountain viewpoints.
  • Enjoy winter photography.

Best time to visit: December to February, depending on snow conditions and activity schedules.

5. Sonamarg, Kashmir – Discover a Snow-Covered Valley

Sonamarg, also known as the Meadow of Gold, is famous for its spectacular mountain scenery and beautiful valleys. During winter, snowfall can transform the surrounding landscape into a striking white setting.

It is an appealing destination for travellers who enjoy nature, photography and peaceful mountain surroundings.

Top Things to Do

  • Enjoy views of the snowy mountains.
  • Take photographs of winter landscapes.
  • Explore accessible scenic areas.
  • Experience local Kashmiri hospitality.

Best time to visit: Winter months when access is available. Check road conditions before travelling.

6. Pahalgam, Kashmir – A Peaceful Winter Escape

Pahalgam is a scenic destination surrounded by rivers, pine forests and beautiful mountain landscapes. In winter, snowfall can create a peaceful atmosphere and picturesque scenery.

Depending on weather and road conditions, visitors may be able to explore popular areas such as Betaab Valley and Aru Valley.

Top Things to Do

  • Enjoy beautiful snowy landscapes.
  • Explore accessible scenic valleys.
  • Try local Kashmiri food.
  • Enjoy the peaceful natural surroundings.

Best time to visit: December to February, subject to local weather and access.

7. Tawang, Arunachal Pradesh – Explore the Snowy Northeast

Tawang is a beautiful winter destination in northeastern India. Known for its monasteries, peaceful surroundings and Himalayan landscapes, it offers a different experience from the popular hill stations of northern India.

During winter, snowfall may occur in the surrounding mountain areas. Sela Pass can experience snow and challenging weather conditions, making local travel updates important before visiting.

Top Things to Do

  • Visit the famous Tawang Monastery.
  • Explore Sela Pass when access permits.
  • Discover local culture and traditions.
  • Take photographs of the mountain scenery.

Best time to visit: December to February for winter scenery, subject to road access and weather conditions.

8. Narkanda, Himachal Pradesh – A Quiet Snowy Escape

Narkanda is a peaceful alternative to some of Himachal Pradesh's busier hill stations. Surrounded by pine forests and mountain scenery, it offers a relaxed winter atmosphere.

The destination can also offer skiing opportunities when snowfall and local conditions are suitable.

Top Things to Do

  • Enjoy seasonal skiing when available.
  • Visit Hatu Peak if access permits.
  • Explore snow-covered forests.
  • Enjoy scenic mountain views.

Best time to visit: December to February, depending on snowfall and road conditions.

9. Dhanaulti, Uttarakhand – A Beautiful Winter Wonderland

Dhanaulti is a peaceful hill station surrounded by forests and mountain landscapes. It is a suitable choice for travellers who prefer a quieter winter holiday away from crowded destinations.

When snowfall occurs, the surrounding forests and hills can become especially scenic. Visitors can enjoy nature, viewpoints and relaxed outdoor experiences.

Top Things to Do

  • Visit Eco Park.
  • Enjoy forest and mountain views.
  • Explore accessible nearby viewpoints.
  • Take winter landscape photographs.

Best time to visit: December to February, depending on snowfall and local conditions.

Tips for Planning Your Winter Snow Holiday in India

Planning ahead can make a winter trip more comfortable and help you prepare for changing mountain weather.

Choose the Right Month

December, January and February are popular winter travel months. January and February can offer snowfall opportunities in several Himalayan destinations, but actual snowfall varies by location and year.

Check Weather Conditions

Snowfall is unpredictable. Check local forecasts, road conditions and destination updates before travelling. Weather can change quickly in mountain regions.

Pack Warm Clothing

Bring thermal layers, insulated jackets, gloves, wool socks and suitable waterproof footwear. Choose clothing that keeps you warm and comfortable during outdoor activities.

Book Accommodation in Advance

Popular winter destinations can become busy during holiday periods. Booking accommodation early can give you more options and help you plan your travel arrangements.

Plan Your Transportation

Heavy snowfall can affect road conditions, transport schedules and access to mountain destinations. Check local updates and allow extra time for travel disruptions.

Follow Safety Guidance

Choose reputable operators for skiing and other adventure activities. Follow local instructions, use appropriate equipment and avoid entering restricted areas.

Winter travel reminder: Snowfall is not guaranteed on specific dates. Check current forecasts, road access, activity schedules and local advisories before travelling to a snowy destination.

Conclusion

India has several beautiful winter destinations, offering everything from exciting snow adventures to peaceful mountain escapes.

Gulmarg, Manali, Auli and Shimla are popular choices for winter scenery and seasonal activities. Sonamarg, Pahalgam, Tawang, Narkanda and Dhanaulti offer other opportunities to enjoy mountain landscapes and winter surroundings.

The places to see snow in India mentioned in this guide provide opportunities to admire breathtaking scenery, discover new experiences and create memorable winter travel moments.

Whether you are planning a family holiday, a romantic getaway or a trip with friends, choose your destination according to your interests, travel dates and available time.

Check weather conditions, transportation, accommodation and activity availability before finalising your plans. With thoughtful preparation, you can enjoy India's beautiful winter landscapes and make your holiday more comfortable.
