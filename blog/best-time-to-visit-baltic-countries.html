Understanding the Baltic Countries' Weather

Estonia, Latvia and Lithuania experience four distinct seasons. Summers are generally mild, while winters can be cold, with snowfall possible across the region. Weather conditions vary throughout the year, and each season offers different opportunities for travellers.

  • Spring (March to May): Temperatures gradually rise, flowers begin to bloom and parks and countryside areas become greener.
  • Summer (June to August): Pleasant temperatures, long daylight hours and outdoor festivals make this a popular travel season.
  • Autumn (September to November): Landscapes turn golden and colourful as temperatures become cooler and visitor numbers generally decrease.
  • Winter (December to February): Cold weather, possible snow, festive decorations and traditional Christmas markets create a seasonal atmosphere.

Understanding these seasonal changes can help you decide when to explore historic attractions, enjoy outdoor activities or experience local celebrations.

Best Seasons to Visit the Baltic Countries

Spring (March to May)

Spring is a pleasant season for travellers who enjoy relaxed sightseeing and exploring nature. As winter comes to an end, parks, gardens and countryside landscapes gradually become greener.

April and May are generally more comfortable for outdoor sightseeing than March. These months are suitable for exploring historic city centres and visiting attractions without the busiest summer crowds.

Top experiences:

  • Explore Tallinn Old Town in Estonia.
  • Visit Riga's parks and gardens in Latvia.
  • Discover the historic streets of Vilnius in Lithuania.
  • Visit Trakai Island Castle.
  • Enjoy countryside photography as nature comes back to life.
Travel tip: May is a good month for sightseeing. Pack light jackets and waterproof clothing because spring showers are possible.

Summer (June to August)

Summer is generally the most popular season to visit the Baltic countries. Temperatures are often around 16°C to 25°C, although warmer days can occur. Longer daylight hours give visitors more time to explore cities, beaches and outdoor attractions.

Top experiences:

  • Explore Estonia's islands and coastal scenery.
  • Relax at Jurmala Beach in Latvia.
  • Visit Palanga's seaside attractions in Lithuania.
  • Attend outdoor events and cultural festivals.
  • Enjoy the coastal landscapes along the Baltic Sea.
Travel tip: June, July and August are excellent for outdoor activities. Summer is also the busiest travel period, so arrange accommodation and transport ahead of time.

Autumn (September to November)

Autumn transforms the Baltic countryside with shades of gold, orange and red. September and early October are particularly appealing for nature lovers and photography enthusiasts.

Temperatures become cooler during this season, and popular attractions are often less crowded than they are in summer. It can be a good time to enjoy a slower city break or explore the countryside.

Top experiences:

  • Visit Gauja National Park in Latvia.
  • Explore the scenic attractions around Sigulda.
  • Enjoy walks through Estonia's forests.
  • Explore Vilnius Old Town.
  • Try traditional Baltic dishes in local restaurants.
Travel tip: September is a good choice for comfortable sightseeing. Bring warm layers and comfortable walking shoes, particularly if you plan to visit parks and nature areas.

Winter (December to February)

Estonia, Latvia and Lithuania have a special atmosphere during winter. Snow-covered streets, festive decorations, traditional Christmas markets and cosy cafés make the season memorable for visitors who enjoy winter destinations.

Tallinn, Riga and Vilnius are especially atmospheric in December, when seasonal decorations and Christmas celebrations bring their historic centres to life.

Top experiences:

  • Visit Tallinn Christmas Market in Estonia.
  • Explore Riga's festive Old Town.
  • See the seasonal decorations around Vilnius Cathedral Square.
  • Enjoy winter photography and city walks.
  • Try local seasonal food and warm drinks.
Travel tip: Winter temperatures can fall well below freezing. Pack an insulated jacket, thermal layers, scarves, gloves and waterproof footwear.

Best Time to Visit Estonia, Latvia and Lithuania

Each Baltic country has its own attractions and seasonal highlights. Your ideal travel dates may depend on which places you want to explore.

Estonia

June to August is a popular time to visit Estonia, especially for exploring Tallinn Old Town, Lahemaa National Park, Pärnu Beach and Saaremaa Island. December is another appealing time for visitors interested in Christmas celebrations and winter scenery.

Latvia

Summer is ideal for exploring Riga's architecture, relaxing at Jurmala Beach and visiting Gauja National Park. Autumn is particularly attractive for countryside photography and enjoying the changing colours of the landscape.

Lithuania

May to September is a good period to explore Vilnius Old Town, Trakai Island Castle, Kaunas and Palanga Beach. December offers a festive atmosphere, particularly around the historic centre of Vilnius.

Essential Travel Tips for Visiting the Baltic Countries

A little preparation can make a multi-country Baltic holiday more comfortable. Keep these practical points in mind before travelling.

  1. Pack appropriate clothing: Choose seasonal clothing, comfortable walking shoes and waterproof jackets. Carry warm layers even during cooler summer days.
  2. Plan your itinerary: Allow enough time to explore all three countries rather than trying to visit too many places in a short period.
  3. Book ahead during busy seasons: Summer and the Christmas period can attract more visitors, so arrange accommodation and transport early.
  4. Choose suitable transport: Buses, trains and rental cars are practical options for travelling between destinations. Check routes and schedules before setting out.
  5. Sample local food: Try Estonian black bread, Latvian grey peas with bacon and Lithuanian cepelinai.
  6. Check visa requirements: Estonia, Latvia and Lithuania are part of the Schengen Area. Check the latest entry requirements based on your nationality and travel circumstances.
  7. Prepare your travel essentials: Carry your passport, travel documents, travel insurance and any personal essentials you may need.
