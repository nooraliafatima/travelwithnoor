Dead Sea Jordan: Best Things to Do and Travel Tips

Discover the best things to do at the Dead Sea, from floating and mud treatments to sunsets, resorts and nearby attractions.

The Dead Sea Jordan is one of the most intriguing nature attractions in Middle East which provides the traveler with a very contrasting experience as compared to the one that a normal beach vacation provides. The Dead Sea is famous due to its extremely salty water, mud with a rich variety of minerals, great landscapes, and a peaceful environment that attracts tourists in need of relaxation, nature, wellness, and memorable photography experiences. Being one of the lowest points on the planet, it offers an impressive landscape with mountains and desert landscape. The destination should be incorporated by tourists who intend to have an extended vacation in Jordan that encompasses Amman, Petra, Wadi Rum, among other attractions under well thought-out Jordan tour packages.

Visit to Dead Sea Jordan can be as lively or as you desire. You will have the opportunity to have the well-known floating experience, have a traditional mud treatment, have a resort spa, take pictures of the surrounding landscape or just enjoy the sunset by the water. Nevertheless, due to the high salinity of the Dead Sea, compared to normal seawater, tourists should take some precautions. Knowing the activities to do, what time to travel, what to carry and how to be comfortable will go a long way in ensuring that you do not end up being caught in unnecessary issues when visiting. When properly prepared the Dead Sea can be one of the most memorable attractions on your Jordan itinerary.

Why Visit the Dead Sea?

The Dead Sea is the place that is characterized by an unusually high amount of salt. With this, individuals are able to move freely on the surface without having to swim as is the case. Experience is among the primary factors that make the destination part of the Jordan plans of travelers.

In addition to floating, the region has mineral-beneficial mud and spa. Hotels and resorts located on the coast usually provide spa treatments, pool, personal beach areas, restaurants and comfortable relaxation areas.

Another great attraction is the scenery around. The blue water combined with rugged mountains, desert and wide open skies make the place a spectacular atmosphere to capture images and have a relaxing time sightseeing.

Float in the Dead Sea

The most popular activity that the visitors would wish to engage in is floating. With high density, the water is highly buoyant and thus you can lie back and rest on the surface with very little force.

The Dead Sea is however not just an ordinary swimming pool. Do not splash, dive into the water gradually. Saltwater will be very uncomfortable when it gets in your mouth or your eyes.

Nor is it better to have yourself shaved just before you visit: unless the slightest irritation of the skin will be stingy when in contact with very salty water. Correspondingly, avoid going into the water with open cuts or wounds.

After getting used to floating, take a pause and appreciate the surreal experience and landscape around you.

Enjoy a Mineral Mud Treatment

Dead Sea mud is another attraction associated with the region. A large number of the visitors loved to put mud on their skin that contained minerals and clean it using fresh water.

A number of resorts and wellness centers offer scheduled mud treatments, and they are a convenient choice of travelers who want to have a relaxing experience. In case you apply mud yourself, take the guidelines given by the beach or resort and do not touch your eyes.

Then wash your body with cold water. The mud treatment experience and floating experience can transform your visit into more of a wellness retreat than a typical sightseeing destination.

Stay at a Dead Sea Resort

Should you have time in your schedule, you may want to spend the night in a Dead Sea resort to have a more relaxed experience. A variety of properties provide a direct access to the beach, as well as swimming pools, restaurants, spa facilities and comfy spaces located near the water.

Overnight stay provides you with greater flexibility. You will be able to spend the afternoon at the beach, spend time at the resort, watch the sunset, have dinner and experience the rest of it the next morning instead of cramming everything into a couple of hours.

When choosing a place to stay, make sure it has access to the beach, towels, showers, and others.

Watch the Sunset

Sunset is one of the most enjoyable times to experience the Dead Sea Jordan. With the sun setting towards the horizon, the mountains and water around can assume gorgeous golden and warm hues.

The scenery provides a great shooting scenery, especially when the shoreline or a resort that commands a view of the water are used. Phone and cameras should be covered to avoid being wet since salt water can destroy electronic devices.

Although photography may not be your primary concern, there is nothing like being next to the water at sunset and relaxing.

Explore Nearby Attractions

The visit of the Dead Sea could be combined with cultural and historical destinations easily constructed in the area. Mount Nebo is a tourist attraction, with a bit of height to provide sweeping views of the surrounding area, and important religious and historical connections.

Another place of interest is Madaba. It features its historic mosaics and offers one a chance to explore the other face of Jordan besides its natural features.

Visitors who have more time can develop a more comprehensive program to include Amman, Petra, Wadi Rum, and other sites. This enables the Dead Sea to be relaxing to supplement the archaeological, cultural and desert experiences of Jordan.

Best Time to Visit

The seasons of spring and autumn are usually good seasons to venture out and explore since the weather may be cooler than during the hottest summer seasons. Winter may also provide good conditions on the appropriate days but the temperature may fluctuate.

It is quite hot in summer and especially during the middle of the day. In case you are there on this season, you should think of scheduling water activities on cooler days in the day and having breaks without being exposed to the sun.

Regardless of the season that you are in, take plenty of water and protect yourself against the sun. The sunshine coupled with dry weather may cause the problem of dehydration when engaging in out-door activities.

What to Pack

Proper packing can help you to have a more comfortable Dead Sea experience. The list of necessities consists of swimwear, water shoes or sandals, sunscreen, sunglasses, hat, drinking water and a change of clothes.

Your phone and other valuables can be taken care of by a waterproof pouch around the water. Carry a towel unless your hotel or resort offers one.

You must also be able to have fresh water to rinse. Floating with removing the salt on your skin relieves you of the residual pain.

Important Safety Tips

The unusual water conditions require some basic precautions. Do not put Dead Sea water in your mouth or eyes and never jump in the water. Be cautious and do not splash unnecessarily.

Spending more time in the water is not advisable. Adhere to the safety guidelines of your beach or resort because things and suggestions may be different.

When using mineral mud or going into the water, be careful with your skin in case it is sensitive or irritated. Anyone who is concerned with the possibility of the highly saline water having any effects on an already existing skin issue ought to consult a relevant professional prior to that.

Close supervision of children should always be taken around the water.

How Long Should You Stay?

A day trip will be a good option provided that you are planning to float, play in the mud, take pictures and spend a few hours relaxing. Overnight is more appropriate to tourists who prefer to move more slowly and have more time to use in the resort facilities and wellness.

Depending on your larger Jordan itinerary, your optimum time will be determined. Visitors to Petra and Wadi Rum might opt to include less Dead Sea tour, whereas those who are interested in relaxation can opt to spend the night.

Conclusion

The Dead Sea Jordan is a place of natural beauty, relaxation, wellness, and intriguing geography all in a single amazing place to visit. Cruising on the salty water, enjoying the mud, spending time in a resort, sunset and sightseeing around the area can provide a diverse and an enjoyable traveling experience. Its peculiar topography is also a refreshing contrast to the antique cities, archaeology sites and desert escapades of Jordan.

With a little prior planning you can visit even better. Wear appropriate clothes, avoid the sun, hydrate, follow local safety measures and have ample time to enjoy the environment. Be it a few hours visit or a night by the water, the Dead Sea Jordan can provide a unique and refreshing experience that you can include in your Jordan trip.
