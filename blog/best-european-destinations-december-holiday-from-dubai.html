Best European Destinations for a December Holiday From Dubai

Discover snowy mountain escapes, beautiful Christmas markets and festive European cities for your December holiday.

Europe Travel Guide · December Holidays

December is a wonderful time to take a break from Dubai and experience the winter charm of Europe. Snow-covered mountains, beautiful Christmas markets, romantic streets and festive celebrations create memorable holiday experiences for travellers. Whether you are planning a family vacation, a romantic getaway or an adventure with friends, exploring the best European destinations for a December holiday from Dubai can make your year-end break special.

European countries offer different experiences during the festive season, from winter landscapes and historic attractions to seasonal food and outdoor activities.

December is also a popular time for UAE residents to plan an international holiday with family and friends. You might dream of seeing snowfall in Switzerland, visiting a traditional Christmas market in Germany or experiencing a snowy adventure in Finland. There are plenty of destinations to consider.

Planning ahead can make your holiday easier, particularly during the busy festive period. You can explore the available European travel options from Dubai and choose an itinerary that suits your interests, budget and travel preferences.

1. Switzerland – Experience the Magic of a Winter Wonderland

Switzerland is one of the most attractive destinations for a December winter holiday. Snow-covered mountains, charming villages and beautiful Alpine scenery create a classic European winter experience. Visitors can enjoy scenic train journeys, winter activities and festive markets while exploring the country's picturesque towns.

Top Attractions

  • Zurich: Explore Christmas markets, festive streets and shopping areas.
  • Lucerne: Discover the beautiful lake, historic bridges and charming old town.
  • Interlaken: Enjoy spectacular views of the surrounding mountains.
  • Jungfraujoch: Experience the famous high-altitude mountain destination and its snowy scenery.
  • Zermatt: Admire views of the iconic Matterhorn mountain.

Best for: Families, couples, honeymooners and adventure enthusiasts.

2. Paris, France – A Romantic Festive Escape

Paris becomes especially atmospheric in December, when festive lights brighten its streets and popular shopping areas. Visitors can enjoy romantic walks, French cuisine, seasonal decorations and some of the world's most recognised attractions.

Top Attractions

  • Eiffel Tower: Enjoy impressive views across the French capital.
  • Louvre Museum: Discover famous artworks and historic collections.
  • Champs-Élysées: Explore shopping areas and seasonal decorations.
  • Montmartre: Wander through artistic streets and charming cafés.
  • Disneyland Paris: Enjoy seasonal entertainment and family attractions.

Best for: Families, couples and travellers interested in shopping, culture and sightseeing.

3. Vienna, Austria – Explore Traditional Christmas Markets

Vienna is famous for its traditional Christmas markets, impressive architecture and festive atmosphere. During December, its historic streets and public squares are decorated with seasonal lights, creating a memorable setting for a winter city break.

Top Attractions

  • Schönbrunn Palace: Explore the historic imperial palace and its seasonal market.
  • St. Stephen's Cathedral: Visit one of Vienna's most recognisable landmarks.
  • Rathausplatz Christmas Market: Experience festive stalls, decorations and seasonal treats.
  • Belvedere Palace: Admire its architecture and art collections.
  • Vienna State Opera: Discover the city's famous performing arts venue.

Visitors can also enjoy traditional Austrian delicacies, warm drinks, handmade gifts and festive performances.

Best for: Families, couples and Christmas market enthusiasts.

4. Prague, Czech Republic – A Fairytale Winter Destination

Prague is a beautiful destination to visit in December, thanks to its medieval architecture, historic bridges and traditional Christmas markets. The Old Town Square becomes a festive gathering place, with seasonal decorations, market stalls and local food.

Top Attractions

  • Charles Bridge: Enjoy views of the Vltava River and the historic city skyline.
  • Prague Castle: Explore one of the city's most important historic landmarks.
  • Old Town Square: Visit the famous Christmas market and admire the surrounding architecture.
  • Astronomical Clock: See the historic clock in the heart of the Old Town.
  • St. Vitus Cathedral: Discover its impressive Gothic architecture.

Best for: Couples, photographers and travellers looking for a relatively affordable European city break.

5. Germany – Experience Traditional Christmas Celebrations

Germany is an excellent choice for travellers who want to experience traditional European Christmas celebrations. Its famous Christmas markets, seasonal food, handmade decorations and beautifully illuminated streets attract visitors from around the world.

Top Attractions

  • Nuremberg: Visit the famous Christkindlesmarkt, one of Germany's best-known Christmas markets.
  • Munich: Explore Marienplatz and its festive market atmosphere.
  • Berlin: Discover seasonal attractions, shopping streets and different Christmas markets.
  • Dresden: Experience the traditional Striezelmarkt.
  • Cologne: Visit the impressive cathedral and nearby Christmas market.

Travellers can enjoy traditional German treats, hot drinks, festive decorations and local crafts while exploring these cities.

Best for: Families, food lovers and travellers who enjoy Christmas markets.

6. Finland – Enjoy a Magical Snowy Holiday

Finland is an exciting destination for travellers dreaming of a white Christmas. Finnish Lapland, particularly the region around Rovaniemi, is popular during winter for its snowy landscapes, Santa Claus Village and outdoor adventures.

Top Attractions and Activities

  • Santa Claus Village: Visit the famous Christmas-themed attraction in Rovaniemi.
  • Northern Lights viewing: Look for the aurora borealis in suitable conditions.
  • Husky sledging: Experience an exciting ride through snowy landscapes.
  • Reindeer rides: Discover a traditional Lapland winter experience.
  • Snowmobile adventures: Explore designated snowy routes with a guided experience.

December offers a special atmosphere in Lapland, although daylight hours are limited. Northern Lights sightings are never guaranteed and depend on weather and solar activity.

Best for: Families with children, couples and adventure seekers.

7. Italy – Discover Culture and Festive Beauty

Italy offers a mix of history, architecture, shopping and delicious food. December can be a good time to explore its famous cities, enjoy seasonal celebrations and visit cultural attractions. Winter weather varies between northern and southern Italy, so travellers can choose destinations based on their preferences.

Top Attractions

  • Rome: Visit the Colosseum, Vatican City and historic city squares.
  • Venice: Explore its canals, historic bridges and romantic gondola experiences.
  • Milan: Discover fashion districts, shopping areas and impressive architecture.
  • Florence: Admire Renaissance architecture, museums and art galleries.
  • Naples: Enjoy traditional Italian cuisine and explore the city's historic streets.

Best for: Couples, families and travellers interested in culture, history and food.

8. Spain – Enjoy a Pleasant European Winter

Spain is a good option for travellers who prefer milder winter weather instead of heavy snowfall. Its cities offer historic sightseeing, shopping, traditional food and seasonal celebrations. Southern destinations such as Seville and Málaga can be particularly appealing for travellers looking for a relatively mild December break.

Top Attractions

  • Barcelona: Visit the Sagrada Família and Park Güell.
  • Madrid: Explore the Royal Palace, Plaza Mayor and festive city streets.
  • Seville: Discover historic architecture and traditional Andalusian culture.
  • Málaga: Enjoy coastal views, historic attractions and seasonal lights.
  • Granada: Visit the famous Alhambra Palace and explore the city's historic neighbourhoods.

Best for: Families, couples and travellers who prefer milder winter temperatures.

Essential Travel Tips for Dubai Residents

Before arranging your European holiday in December, consider these practical travel tips to make your journey more comfortable.

  1. Book early: December is a busy travel season, particularly around Christmas and New Year. Book flights and accommodation in advance where possible.
  2. Check visa requirements: Review Schengen visa requirements based on your nationality and individual circumstances. UAE residence alone does not determine visa-free entry.
  3. Pack winter clothing: Bring warm jackets, thermal layers, gloves, scarves and comfortable shoes suitable for cold or wet conditions.
  4. Plan your budget: Consider flights, hotels, meals, local transport, sightseeing tickets and shopping expenses.
  5. Choose the right destination: Consider Switzerland and Finland for snowy experiences, Germany and Austria for Christmas markets, and Spain for relatively mild winter weather.
  6. Check seasonal opening times: Some attractions, restaurants and transport services may operate on different schedules during Christmas and New Year.
  7. Allow enough travel time: Avoid planning too many cities into a short holiday. Winter weather and shorter daylight hours can affect sightseeing schedules.
Important: December weather varies across Europe. Check the local forecast shortly before departure and confirm Christmas market dates and attraction opening hours for your travel year.
