Best Scenic Train Journeys to Experience in Japan

Discover beautiful railway routes through Japan's mountains, forests, coastlines and countryside.

Japan is a destination where travelling between places can be just as exciting as exploring the attractions themselves. The country has a remarkable railway network connecting major cities, peaceful villages, scenic coastlines and mountain valleys. Some train journeys offer beautiful views of rivers and forests, while others pass through flower fields, traditional towns and impressive mountain landscapes.

For travellers looking beyond the usual sightseeing attractions, scenic train journeys in Japan provide a unique way to experience the country's natural beauty and local culture. From historic sightseeing trains to comfortable observation carriages, these railway experiences make the journey itself an important part of a holiday.

Travellers planning a wider trip can also explore Japan tour options for destination inspiration and travel planning. Here are nine scenic train journeys worth considering when exploring Japan.

1. Sagano Romantic Train – Kyoto

The Sagano Romantic Train is one of the most popular sightseeing railway experiences near Kyoto. Running between Saga Torokko Station and Kameoka Torokko Station, the train follows the beautiful Hozu River valley and offers views of wooded hills, rocky landscapes and the winding river.

The journey is particularly attractive during spring, when fresh greenery and seasonal blossoms brighten the surroundings. Autumn is another popular period, as the surrounding hills turn shades of red, orange and yellow. The open-sided carriage options also allow passengers to enjoy the scenery more closely.

2. Kurobe Gorge Railway – Toyama

The Kurobe Gorge Railway is a memorable experience for travellers who enjoy dramatic mountain scenery. Located in Toyama Prefecture, the railway travels through the Kurobe Gorge, following the Kurobe River across bridges, tunnels and steep valley landscapes.

Passengers can admire forest-covered slopes, deep ravines, river views and impressive mountain surroundings along the route. Autumn is especially popular because the changing foliage creates colourful scenery across the gorge. Check the railway's operating calendar before planning your visit, as services are seasonal and may be affected by weather or maintenance.

3. Resort Shirakami – Aomori and Akita

Resort Shirakami offers a scenic railway experience along the Gono Line in northern Japan. Connecting areas of Aomori and Akita, the route is known for its views of the Sea of Japan, coastal landscapes and the forested surroundings of the Shirakami region.

Large windows make it easier for passengers to appreciate the scenery during the journey. Depending on the service, visitors may also experience local food, regional cultural performances and other onboard features. It is a wonderful option for travellers interested in exploring a less familiar part of Japan.

4. Hakone Tozan Railway – Kanagawa

The Hakone Tozan Railway is a charming mountain railway that connects Hakone-Yumoto with Gora. It travels through wooded hills and steep mountain terrain, using switchback sections to manage the changes in elevation.

During summer, hydrangeas bloom along parts of the route, adding colour to the railway scenery. The train also provides access to popular Hakone attractions, including hot spring areas and places around Lake Ashi. It is a good choice for visitors who want to combine a scenic train ride with a relaxing mountain escape.

5. Furano Biei Norokko Train – Hokkaido

The Furano and Biei areas of Hokkaido are known for their open countryside, rolling hills and colourful flower fields. The Furano Biei Norokko Train provides a relaxed way to enjoy these landscapes during its operating season.

Summer is particularly attractive, when lavender fields, colourful flower gardens and green hills create beautiful views. The slower sightseeing service allows passengers to enjoy the countryside and take photographs along the route. Since the train operates seasonally, check the current timetable before arranging your visit.

6. Yufuin no Mori – Kyushu

Yufuin no Mori is a popular sightseeing train connecting Hakata with Yufuin in Kyushu. Its distinctive green exterior and comfortable interiors create a pleasant atmosphere for the journey through the region's countryside.

Passengers can enjoy views of forests, rivers and mountain landscapes along the route. Yufuin itself is a well-known hot spring town with traditional streets, local shops and relaxing onsen experiences. This train journey is a good option for travellers who want to combine scenic railway travel with a visit to a Japanese hot spring destination.

7. Oigawa Railway Steam Locomotive – Shizuoka

The Oigawa Railway in Shizuoka offers a nostalgic railway experience for visitors interested in traditional locomotives and historic train travel. Its steam locomotive services, when operating, provide an opportunity to experience an older style of Japanese railway journey.

The route passes through quiet rural communities, green tea fields, rivers and bridges. The traditional train atmosphere and surrounding countryside make the journey particularly appealing to railway enthusiasts and photographers. Steam locomotive operations and available routes can change, so check the railway's latest service information before travelling.

8. JR Koumi Line – Yamanashi and Nagano

The JR Koumi Line is a scenic railway route that passes through parts of Yamanashi and Nagano Prefectures. It is known for its peaceful highland landscapes and views of the Yatsugatake Mountains.

The journey takes passengers through rural communities, open fields and mountain scenery. Nobeyama Station is one of the notable stops along the line and is recognised for its high elevation. The route is particularly appealing to nature lovers and photographers who enjoy exploring quieter parts of Japan.

9. JR Yosan Line – Shikoku

The JR Yosan Line is a wonderful way to explore parts of Shikoku by rail. The route connects several towns and offers a mix of coastal scenery, rural landscapes and access to regional attractions.

One of its best-known scenic locations is Shimonada Station, which is famous for its views towards the Seto Inland Sea. The station has become a popular photography spot because of its open waterfront setting. Travellers can enjoy a relaxed railway experience while discovering the towns and landscapes of Shikoku.

Best Time to Experience Scenic Train Journeys in Japan

Japan's railway scenery changes throughout the year, making each season attractive in its own way. The best time for a scenic train journey depends on the route, weather conditions and landscapes you want to experience.

  • Spring (March to May): Fresh greenery and seasonal blossoms create beautiful views along selected routes. Cherry blossom timing varies by region and year.
  • Summer (June to August): Flower fields, green countryside and mountain scenery are highlights, particularly in Hokkaido. Some sightseeing trains operate mainly during this period.
  • Autumn (September to November): Colourful foliage makes mountain and forest routes especially attractive. Popular examples include the Sagano Romantic Train and Kurobe Gorge Railway.
  • Winter (December to February): Snow-covered landscapes can create beautiful scenery in northern and mountainous regions, although some sightseeing services may have seasonal closures.

Before travelling, check the official railway timetable, seasonal operating dates and local weather conditions. Some sightseeing trains run only during selected months, while others may change their schedules depending on the season.

Essential Tips for Planning a Scenic Train Journey in Japan

Planning ahead can make your railway experience more comfortable and enjoyable. Keep these useful suggestions in mind before starting your journey.

  • Reserve popular trains early: Well-known sightseeing trains can fill up quickly, particularly during cherry blossom and autumn foliage seasons.
  • Choose your seat carefully: Check the route and seating layout to select a position with good views of the main scenery.
  • Confirm seasonal services: Check operating dates, departure times and possible maintenance closures before making reservations.
  • Keep your camera ready: Scenic views can appear quickly, so keep your camera or phone accessible during the journey.
  • Explore nearby attractions: Combine train journeys with local markets, traditional restaurants, temples and hot spring destinations.
  • Allow time for connections: Leave enough time between trains, especially when travelling through rural areas with less frequent services.
  • Check luggage arrangements: Review the luggage space and restrictions of the train you plan to use, particularly on smaller sightseeing services.

Conclusion

Japan offers memorable railway experiences for travellers who want to explore more than its major cities. From the river valleys of Kyoto and the mountain scenery of Toyama to the coastal landscapes of northern Japan, each scenic route provides a different view of the country.

The Sagano Romantic Train, Kurobe Gorge Railway, Resort Shirakami and Yufuin no Mori are just a few examples of the journeys that make railway travel an enjoyable part of a Japanese holiday. Whether you prefer mountain landscapes, colourful flower fields, historic locomotives or peaceful coastal views, there is a scenic route to suit different interests.

Adding one or more of these scenic train journeys in Japan to your itinerary can make your trip more rewarding. With advance reservations, a suitable travel season and a well-planned route, you can discover a memorable side of Japan beyond its famous cities.
