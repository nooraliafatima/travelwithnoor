Malaysia in December: Best Places to Visit and Things to Do

Explore Malaysia's vibrant cities, tropical islands, cultural attractions and hill stations during the festive December season.

Home / Blog / Malaysia in December
Asia Travel Guide · December Holidays

December is a wonderful time to plan a holiday in Malaysia, especially for travellers who enjoy exploring beautiful destinations, experiencing different cultures and discovering exciting attractions. Modern cities, tropical islands, historic towns and cool hill stations make the country appealing to different types of travellers.

Malaysia is known for its diverse food, shopping destinations and welcoming atmosphere. December also brings festive celebrations, with major cities featuring Christmas decorations, entertainment and New Year events. Whether you are planning a family holiday, a romantic getaway or a fun trip with friends, Malaysia offers a variety of experiences.

Exploring Malaysia in December allows travellers to visit popular destinations while enjoying the festive atmosphere. However, December falls within the northeast monsoon season, which brings heavy rainfall to parts of the country, particularly the east coast. Choosing destinations according to local weather conditions can help make your trip more comfortable.

Western destinations such as Kuala Lumpur, Penang and Langkawi are often considered for December itineraries, although rain can still occur. Travellers can explore famous attractions, experience local culture and enjoy the country's different landscapes with a well-organised Malaysia travel itinerary.

1. Explore Kuala Lumpur

Kuala Lumpur is an excellent starting point for a Malaysian holiday. The capital city combines modern architecture, cultural attractions, shopping centres and delicious street food. It is also a lively destination during December, when shopping malls and public spaces feature festive decorations and seasonal events.

Popular Attractions

  • Petronas Twin Towers: Admire Malaysia's famous twin skyscrapers and enjoy impressive views of the city from the observation areas.
  • Batu Caves: Visit the colourful Hindu temple complex and see its enormous golden statue.
  • KL Tower: Enjoy panoramic views of Kuala Lumpur from one of the city's best-known landmarks.
  • Bukit Bintang: Explore shopping centres, restaurants and entertainment venues.
  • Merdeka Square: Discover an important historical area surrounded by colonial-era architecture.

December adds a festive atmosphere to Kuala Lumpur, with shopping malls, restaurants and entertainment areas decorated for Christmas and New Year celebrations.

2. Relax on Langkawi Island

Langkawi is a beautiful island destination known for sandy beaches, clear coastal waters and a relaxed atmosphere. December is generally considered a favourable period for visiting this western coastal destination, although occasional showers are still possible.

Experiences to Enjoy

  • Pantai Cenang: Relax on the beach and explore its restaurants and cafés.
  • Langkawi Sky Bridge: Enjoy impressive views of the surrounding mountains, forest and sea.
  • Langkawi Cable Car: Take a scenic ride above the island's green landscapes.
  • Island hopping: Explore nearby islands and coastal scenery on a boat trip, depending on sea conditions.
  • Sunset cruises: Enjoy a relaxing evening on the water while watching the sunset.

Langkawi is a good option for couples, families and groups looking for a combination of relaxation and outdoor activities.

3. Discover Penang's Cultural Attractions

Penang is known for its historic architecture, colourful streets, cultural attractions and traditional food. Its capital, George Town, is particularly popular among travellers interested in heritage and local culture.

Must-Visit Attractions

  • George Town: Explore heritage buildings, traditional shop houses and colourful street art.
  • Penang Hill: Enjoy beautiful views across the island and surrounding landscapes.
  • Kek Lok Si Temple: Visit one of the region's best-known Buddhist temple complexes.
  • Clan Jetties: Learn about traditional waterfront communities and their history.
  • Penang Street Art: Discover colourful murals and creative artwork throughout George Town.

Food is another reason to visit Penang. Local favourites include nasi kandar, asam laksa and char kway teow. The island is an excellent destination for travellers who want to combine cultural sightseeing with food experiences.

4. Enjoy the Beauty of Cameron Highlands

Cameron Highlands is a peaceful hill destination known for its cooler climate, green tea plantations, strawberry farms and beautiful mountain scenery. It offers a refreshing change from Malaysia's tropical lowland cities.

Things to Do

  • Visit tea plantations and enjoy freshly prepared tea.
  • Explore strawberry farms and pick fresh strawberries when available.
  • Discover colourful flower gardens and nurseries.
  • Visit local markets to shop for regional products and souvenirs.
  • Enjoy scenic drives through the countryside and rolling hills.
Travel tip: Cameron Highlands can experience frequent rainfall in December. Carry an umbrella or rain jacket and check local weather conditions before planning outdoor activities.

5. Explore Historical Malacca

Malacca, also known as Melaka, is an interesting destination for travellers who want to learn about Malaysian history and culture. Its historic architecture, museums, heritage streets and lively markets make it a rewarding place to explore.

Popular Attractions

  • Dutch Square: Admire the distinctive red colonial buildings and historic surroundings.
  • A Famosa: Explore the remains of a Portuguese fortress.
  • Jonker Street: Discover local cuisine, souvenirs, antiques and traditional shops.
  • Stadthuys: Learn about the city's colonial history through this well-known historic building.
  • Malacca River Cruise: Enjoy a relaxing boat ride through the historic city centre.

A day trip to Malacca can be a useful addition to a Malaysian holiday, particularly for travellers staying in Kuala Lumpur.

6. Experience Genting Highlands

Genting Highlands is a popular mountain resort offering entertainment, shopping, restaurants and attractions for different age groups. Its cooler climate makes it a refreshing escape from the warmer conditions in Kuala Lumpur.

Activities to Enjoy

  • Genting SkyWorlds: Experience themed attractions and outdoor rides.
  • Awana SkyWay: Enjoy a scenic cable car journey through the mountain landscape.
  • Chin Swee Caves Temple: Visit this impressive temple surrounded by forested mountain scenery.
  • Shopping centres: Explore international brands, retail stores and restaurants.
  • Indoor entertainment: Enjoy activities and attractions suitable for different age groups.

December is a busy holiday period at Genting Highlands. Booking accommodation and checking attraction availability in advance can help you avoid unnecessary delays.

7. Best Things to Do in Malaysia in December

Beyond visiting popular destinations, Malaysia offers a variety of experiences that can make a December holiday memorable.

Enjoy Water Activities

Depending on the destination and sea conditions, visitors can enjoy activities such as snorkelling, kayaking and swimming. Always check local weather and marine conditions before arranging boat trips or water activities.

Discover Malaysian Cuisine

Explore local restaurants and street markets to try traditional dishes such as nasi lemak, satay and roti canai. Food is an important part of Malaysia's cultural identity, and each region has its own specialities.

Enjoy Festive Shopping

Visit shopping malls in Kuala Lumpur and other major cities to see Christmas decorations, seasonal promotions and New Year celebrations. December can be a lively time for shopping and entertainment.

Explore Nature

Discover botanical gardens, rainforest areas and national parks. Check local weather conditions and follow safety advice before planning hikes or outdoor excursions.

Visit Night Markets

Explore local night markets to shop for souvenirs, traditional products and affordable street food. These markets also offer an opportunity to experience everyday Malaysian culture.

8. Important Travel Tips for December

Planning ahead can make your Malaysian holiday more comfortable, particularly because December is both a busy travel period and part of the northeast monsoon season.

  • Check weather forecasts: Review the weather for each destination before finalising your itinerary.
  • Be careful with east coast island trips: Heavy rain and rough seas can affect boat services and island activities during the northeast monsoon.
  • Pack suitable clothing: Carry comfortable lightweight clothes, an umbrella, a rain jacket and suitable walking shoes.
  • Book in advance: December is a popular holiday month, so arrange flights and accommodation early.
  • Check entry requirements: Review passport validity and the latest entry requirements applicable to your nationality.
  • Respect local customs: Follow appropriate dress codes and visitor guidelines when entering religious sites.
  • Plan transportation: Check travel times and transport connections between cities to make the most of your holiday.
Important monsoon note: The northeast monsoon can bring substantial rainfall to Malaysia's east coast between November and March. Island and coastal activities may be disrupted. Check local advisories and boat schedules before travelling.

Conclusion

Malaysia is an excellent destination for a December holiday, offering a combination of natural beauty, cultural attractions, shopping and adventure. From exploring Kuala Lumpur's impressive skyline and relaxing on Langkawi's beaches to discovering Penang's heritage and enjoying the cooler climate of Cameron Highlands, each destination offers something different.

Although some parts of the country experience monsoon rainfall during December, choosing suitable destinations and planning activities around local weather conditions can help you enjoy a memorable trip.

If you are considering a December holiday in Malaysia, it can be a wonderful opportunity to enjoy a year-end break with family, friends or your partner. With a well-planned itinerary, you can discover the country's landscapes, food, cultural attractions and festive atmosphere.
